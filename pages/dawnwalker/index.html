Narrative

Story & Setting

Set in the year 1347 in Vale Sangora, a fictional kingdom in the Carpathian Mountains ravaged by the Black Death. The Vrakhiri — ancient vampires and creatures of the night — have emerged from the shadows, exploiting humanity's weakness to claim the power and freedom denied to them for centuries. The ancient Vrakhiri lord Brencis — born the Roman noble Caeso Burrienus Laurentius in AD 131 — rules with an iron fist, enforcing servitude and "blood taxes" on the surviving population.

You play as Coen, a young man caught between two worlds. A turning ritual performed by the Vrakhiri went wrong, leaving him half human, half Vrakhiri: a Dawnwalker. Human by day, vampire by night. Coen must save his sister Lunka, consumed by the plague, avenge his family, and decide whether to cling to his humanity or embrace his cursed vampiric powers.

He has 30 days and 30 nights (in game time) before the Blood Mass — an ominous event triggered when time runs out. Exploring the world does not advance time — only completing quests does. Each quest carries a clear "time cost." There is no hard game-over timer, but wasting time means "Coen's family may suffer the consequences." The developers have confirmed that 80% of all content can be completed in a single playthrough, lasting roughly 50 to 70 hours.

In addition, recent updates from the development team strongly hint at lycanthropy — werewolves — alongside the vampires, further expanding the bestiary and lore of the world of Vale Sangora.

"Guard your family while you become what you hate."
— The Guardian (Aug 19, 2026), on Coen's central dilemma
"So non-linear that you can align with the human rebellion or finish the game without ever meeting them. You will not have time for everyone."
— Mateusz Tomaszkiewicz, Creative Director, Rebel Wolves

🌗 Day & Night Duality

Two completely distinct gameplay loops with unique abilities, mysteries and problem-solving approaches. The world reacts dynamically to your actions and playstyle as day turns to night.

🔀 Narrative Sandbox

Fully non-linear. Quests can be completed in any order, skipped, or left undiscovered. You can align with the human rebellion or finish the game without ever encountering them.

💀 Permanent Consequences

Freedom to kill off many NPCs — including really important ones. Every action and inaction shapes the world. Multiple endings based on your choices.

❤️ Romance

Confirmed by the creative director. Build relationships with characters throughout your journey. Romance options woven into the narrative.

Core Systems

Gameplay & Mechanics

The Blood of Dawnwalker is a single-player, story-focused open-world action RPG with a deliberately smaller-scale open world designed to foster deep immersion — lush forests, vast plains, treacherous swamps, steep mountaintops and medieval settlements.

Built on Unreal Engine 5, the game features advanced rendering, dynamic day/night transitions and handcrafted environments inspired by Central and Southeastern European medieval folklore.

⏳ Time Pressure

30 in-game days and nights to save your family. Every quest has a time cost. No hard game-over timer, but wasting time has real consequences for Coen's loved ones.

🧛 Blood Craving System

Using vampiric skills affects Coen's human side, tied to health and hunger. Losing health increases the risk of losing control — triggering bad consequences or permanently locking questlines.

🌀 Planeshift

Breaks gravity to walk on walls and ceilings. A traversal power that lets you navigate the world in unexpected ways, access hidden areas and gain tactical advantages in combat.

👻 Shadowstep

Short-distance teleportation through shadows. Move instantly, escape dangerous situations, or close the gap with enemies in the blink of an eye.

📖 Three Skill Trees

Separate trees for Human perks, Vrakhiri perks and Shared perks — customize Coen's growth depending on whether you lean into humanity or monstrosity.

🔮 Ritual Magic

Raw, ritual-based magic tied to blood, heat and air. Casting requires channeling occult carvings and runes — hexes like Burning Blood boil enemy blood for devastating damage over time.

🔍 Investigation & Detective Skills

Day form lets you speak to the dead and investigate crime scenes. Detective work unlocks new information, dialogue options and quest progression — critical to solving Vale Sangora's mysteries.

🩸 Blood Drinking

Night form allows you to drink the blood of NPCs — a confirmed gameplay mechanic that ties into the Blood Craving system and affects your humanity.

✦ ☠ ✦

Dual Nature

Combat System

Two entirely distinct combat styles — each form has unique strengths and weaknesses, and your tactics must adapt as the sun sets or rises. The Community Bulletin Board #10 (July 2026) went deep on the design: inspired by Historical European Martial Arts, recorded with motion capture and tuned by the animators. Each attack has three phases — acceleration, anticipation and strike — so it's accessible yet deep.

Concept art — a duel at dawn. Concept art by Damian Bajowski · CBB #10 "Swordmastery" (Jul 29, 2026)

☀ Human Form (Day)

  • Sword combat based on authentic medieval fighting styles (HEMA)
  • Short, long and greatswords — each with its own rhythm
  • Raw, ritual-based magic (blood, heat, air)
  • Occult carvings and runes for spellcasting
  • Hexes: Burning Blood and other devastating spells
  • Books that unlock new techniques and passives
  • Strategic positioning and tactical combat

🌙 Vrakhiri Form (Night)

  • Claws and enhanced supernatural strength
  • Dark powers available only after sunset
  • Planeshift — walk on walls and ceilings
  • Shadowstep — short-distance teleportation
  • Brutal finishers (decapitation, etc.)
  • Blood Craving risk — losing control has consequences
  • Speak to the dead and investigate crime scenes
  • Drink NPC blood — ties into the Blood Craving system

⚡ Omniattack / Omniblock

Adaptive system: one button to attack or block in any direction, with the game automatically picking the right response. Ideal for players who want action without losing a sense of rhythm.

🎯 Directional Tactical Mode

For purists: read incoming attacks, land the perfect parry and finish with ripostes. Rewards precision, timing and execution. Every encounter is a deadly exchange where positioning and commitment matter.

🪓 More than swords

Beyond the three sword families, the arsenal includes axes and other weapons found along the way… "and maybe a shovel." — Rebel Wolves, CBB #10

Hands-On Impressions

Several outlets had the chance to play 4+ hours of The Blood of Dawnwalker at IGN First, GamingTrend, GameRant and Gamescom 2025. Here's what they found.

👍 What Stands Out

  • Impeccable atmosphere — "dripping" world design with cinematic-level detail
  • Living environments — slum streets, puddles, mud patches that feel tangible
  • Distinct districts — the city shifts aesthetics, architecture and NPC behavior per area
  • Smooth combat animations — natural movements, fluid transitions between day/night forms
  • Outstanding voice acting — "I want Coen to read me a bedtime story" (CGMagazine)
  • Stunning scale — the cathedral interior alone is breathtakingly detailed
  • Meaningful choices — the time-as-currency system makes every quest feel consequential
  • Romance confirmed — relationship building with characters, including a local witch
  • Customizable camera — adjustable distance with separate battle/exploration presets
  • Difficulty options — adjustable for different player preferences

⚠️ Areas to Improve

  • Target switching — finicky when juggling multiple enemies in combat
  • Disengaging from combat — camera stays locked on target even when you try to leave
  • Eye animation — eyes move but feel disconnected from body/voice emotion during dialogue
  • Hair rendering — hairlines and beards not yet polished where they meet the body
  • Face animation — still a work in progress compared to the rest of the visual fidelity

💤 PC Graphics & Performance

The big technical question: ray tracing? Short answer: no. Despite running on Unreal Engine 5, The Blood of Dawnwalker has no ray tracing or path tracing — and therefore no ray reconstruction either (an RT-specific technology). Lighting is achieved with classic techniques and still looks very good.

🔦Ray Tracing / Path TracingNo. There is no ray tracing in the game.
🌀Ray ReconstructionNot applicable — a DLSS feature that only exists alongside ray tracing.
⚡NVIDIA DLSS 4Yes, with frame generation (FG). The bundled library points to DLSS 4.
🧠AMD FSRYes. Per the community, FSR 4 Balanced performs very well; FSR 2/3 Balanced not so much.
🎮Intel XeSSYes, supported.
"At the Ultra preset, the RTX 5070 Ti hits ~54 fps native at 1440p and jumps to ~102 fps with DLSS Quality + frame generation. No ray tracing, but the image and performance look very good."
— Sportskeeda, PC benchmarks on RTX 5070 Ti (Aug 31, 2026)

Steam Deck: Steam Deck HQ reports (Aug 2026) are cautious — with FSR active, internal resolution drops well below native 800p and the image suffers. Sources: Rebel Wolves (final requirements, Aug 18) · VideoCardz · PC Gamer · DSO Gaming · Sportskeeda · PCGamesHardware · r/nvidia.

Beyond the first chapter

The Dawnwalker Saga

During Summer Game Fest 2026, Rebel Wolves revealed that The Blood of Dawnwalker is only the beginning. A CGI teaser showed Coen in a modern 21st-century setting, offering a glimpse into where the saga's story ultimately leads.

Future games in the franchise will span centuries, continents and cultures around the world, introducing new locations, antagonists and stories. However, the team has been very clear on one point:

📖 Self-Contained Story

The Blood of Dawnwalker has a definitive and complete ending. No cliffhangers, no unresolved plot threads, no time travel. Coen and Brencis's story reaches its conclusion.

🌍 Sagas Across Eras

Each future game will be a standalone entry with its own story, set in a different era, location and culture. Coen travels through time as an immortal Dawnwalker.

🔍 Hidden Breadcrumbs

Attentive players will find saga lore breadcrumbs hidden in the first game, connecting to the broader plot that will unfold in future installments.

"I have a feeling that today's presentation may have surprised many of you who did not expect Coen's saga to move into modern times. However, I would like to emphasize that each installment in the series will be a standalone game with its own story, without narrative cliffhangers, unresolved plot threads, or time travel ideas."
— Konrad Tomaszkiewicz, Game Director & Co-Founder, Rebel Wolves
"I love the potential that lies within Coen, as well as his powers and abilities as a Dawnwalker. His journey begins in a dark 14th-century fantasy world… The next installments of his saga will bring new stories and new characters, but they will take place in different eras, locations, and cultures."
— Jakub Szamałek, Narrative Director, Rebel Wolves

Source: Summer Game Fest 2026 · Rebel Wolves × Bandai Namco Entertainment (June 2026)

Behind the Game

About Rebel Wolves

Rebel Wolves is a Warsaw, Poland-based studio founded by key veterans of CD Projekt Red, including former The Witcher 3 creative directors Konrad Tomaszkiewicz and Mateusz Tomaszkiewicz.

The Blood of Dawnwalker is their debut title and is described as "the first chapter of a brand new dark fantasy role-playing saga." The studio has drawn inspiration from pen-and-paper RPGs, medieval folklore and experimental narrative design to create what they envision as a deeply immersive, consequential RPG experience.

The game is published by Bandai Namco Entertainment and launches simultaneously on PC, PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S on September 3, 2026.

"Every action — and inaction — shapes the world around you. The world waits for no one. And neither does the villain."
